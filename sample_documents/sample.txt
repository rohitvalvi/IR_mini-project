Artificial intelligence is increasingly used to help people find and understand information.
Document summarization reduces a long document to a shorter version while preserving its main ideas.
Extractive systems select existing sentences, whereas abstractive systems generate new wording.
Modern transformer models can use context to produce fluent summaries, but they may still omit
details or introduce factual errors. A reliable application should therefore show the source
text, explain its limitations, and let users review the generated result.
